Research Interests

Condensed matter physics using synchrotron-based techniques.

Synchrotron Techniques

Utilizing synchrotron light sources to probe the structural and electronic properties of condensed matter systems with high energy and spatial resolution.

Electronic Structure

Investigating the electronic structure of correlated electron systems and novel materials using X-ray spectroscopy and related techniques.

Quantum Materials

Studying emergent phenomena in quantum materials, including strongly correlated systems, topological phases, and unconventional superconductors.
